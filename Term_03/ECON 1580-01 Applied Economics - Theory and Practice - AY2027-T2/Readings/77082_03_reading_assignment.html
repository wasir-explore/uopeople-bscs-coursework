Unit 1 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:

  1. What are the defining characteristics of traditional, command, market, and mixed economic systems? 
  2. How do different economic systems handle resource allocation and scarcity? Consider the strengths and weaknesses of each system. 
  3. What roles do governments and businesses play in influencing economic outcomes? 
  4. In what ways do geopolitics and trade policies affect the global economy? 
  5. How do mixed economies strike a balance between market freedom and government intervention? Consider the pros and cons of this approach. 

Read

  1. Greenlaw, S., Shapiro, D., & MacDonald, D. (2022). Principles of Economics (3e ed.). https://openstax.org/books/principles-economics-3e/pages/1-introduction
    • Read Chapter 1: Welcome to Economics
      • Section 1.1 – What is Economics and Why Is It Important  
        • Section 1.1 explains economics as the study of decision-making in the face of scarcity. It covers how scarcity influences choices and introduces the concept of labor specialization to increase productivity. The section also highlights the importance of data, like from the FRED database, in understanding economic issues.
      • Section 1.2 - Microeconomics and Macroeconomics
        • Section 1.2 explains that microeconomics focuses on individual decisions, like those of consumers and businesses, while macroeconomics looks at the broader economy, including growth and unemployment. Both perspectives are connected, with micro decisions impacting macroeconomic outcomes. The section also highlights the roles of fiscal and monetary policy in managing the economy.
      • Section 1.3 - How Economists Use Theories and Models to Understand Economic Issues
        • Section 1.3 explains how economists use theories and models to simplify complex economic issues. The circular flow diagram is introduced, showing how households and firms interact in goods, services, and labor markets. Economists apply these models to analyze and understand real-world economic behavior.
      • Section 1.4 - How To Organize Economies: An Overview of Economic Systems
        • Section 1.4 covers three economic systems: traditional (based on customs), command (government-controlled), and market (driven by supply and demand). It highlights that most economies today are mixed, combining elements of these systems. The section explains that though globalization has boosted trade and interconnectedness, it faces resistance due to concerns about job loss and inequality. 
  2. Understanding Hybrid Public-Private Partnerships: a model for delivering infrastructure. (2024, October 14). World Bank. https://www.worldbank.org/en/topic/sustainableinfrastructurefinance/brief/understanding-hybrid-public-private-partnerships-a-model-for-delivering-infrastructure license by CC 4.0
        • This article explores Hybrid Public-Private Partnerships (PPPs) as a model for infrastructure development, combining government funding, private sector efficiency, and World Bank support to enhance service delivery. By sharing financial risks and leveraging concessional financing, Hybrid PPPs help governments attract investment, improve affordability, and promote sustainable development in low-income regions. 
  3. Edmond, C. (2024, December 13). Geopolitics and trade policy are becoming intertwined. Here’s how it’s impacting the global economy. World Economic Forum. https://www.weforum.org/stories/2024/12/global-trade-geopolitics-uncertainty-economic-policy/ license by CC 4.0
        • This article explores the impact of geopolitics on trade policies as nations prioritize security, sustainability, and reduced dependencies amidst growing fragmentation. Experts emphasize trade's role in addressing global challenges while highlighting the complexities of reshaping supply chains and maintaining interconnectedness in a divided world.

    Additional Readings

    1. Ruzicka, J. (2024, November 25). Rethinking trade and cooperation to navigate global shocks. World Economic Forum. https://www.weforum.org/stories/2024/11/rethinking-trade-cooperation-navigate-global-shocks/ license by CC 4.0
      • This article examines the global economy's challenges, such as geopolitical conflicts, supply chain disruptions, and protectionist policies like friendshoring and trade wars, which risk undermining growth. It advocates for balanced approaches, including supply chain diversification, energy independence, and global cooperation, to ensure long-term stability and shared prosperity.
    2. World Bank Group. (2025). Global economic prospects: Chapter 1. In World Bank Publications (pp. 1-45). https://doi.org/10.1596/978-1-4648-2147-9 license by CC 4.0 
      • This chapter on Global Outlook highlights stabilizing global growth but warns that past economic shocks, policy uncertainty, and risks like trade disruptions and climate change hinder long-term recovery. It stresses the need for decisive policies to safeguard trade, manage debt, and address challenges like human capital and food insecurity for sustainable growth.

    Watch

    1. The Digital Classroom Experience. (2024, November 19). Identifying and describing different types of economic systems [Video]. YouTube.
      • This video explains the four economic systems—traditional, command, market, and mixed—and how they manage resources, from customs-based practices to government control, consumer-driven markets, and blended approaches balancing freedom and social needs.
    2. Professor Dave Explains. (2022, July 27). The purpose of mixed economies [Video]. YouTube.
      • This video explains mixed economies, where markets blend with government intervention to address societal needs, protect rights, ensure fairness, and fund public projects. It uses examples, like buying milk, to show government involvement in everyday transactions.
    3. Taylor J. (2020, October 9). Creative Thinking vs Critical thinking [Video]. YouTube. 
      • This video differentiates between critical thinking, which involves logical consideration from various perspectives, and creative thinking, focused on generating new ideas. Combining these skills helps arrive at clearer and more innovative solutions. In business studies, the synergy between critical thinking and creative thinking is essential for making well-informed decisions, fostering innovation, and addressing complex challenges.
