Unit 2 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:

  1. Discuss the concept of signal encoding and decoding in data transmission.
  2. Explore the advantages and limitations of microwave transmission, radio transmission, and infrared transmission as unguided transmission media.
  3. Compare and contrast guided and unguided transmission media in terms of their characteristics and suitability for different network environments.
  4. Propose a combination of multiplexing techniques and spread spectrum modulation to optimize channel utilization while ensuring reliable data transmission for all users.

    • View the online book.
      • Read Chapter 6 – Links
        • Section 6.2 – Time Division Multiplexing
        Time-Division Multiplexing (TDM) is a technique used in telecommunications to transmit multiple signals over a single communication channel by allocating specific time slots to each signal. This enables efficient utilization of the channel's bandwidth, allowing multiple users to share the same physical medium for data transmission.
