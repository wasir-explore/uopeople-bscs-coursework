Unit 1 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:

  1. What are the key components of data communication, and how do they facilitate the exchange of information between devices?
  2. Compare and contrast the characteristics of:
    • Local Area Networks (LANs),
    • Wide Area Networks (WANs),
    • Metropolitan Area Networks (MANs),
    • Personal Area Networks (PANs), and
    • Campus Area Networks (CANs).
  3. Discuss the role of each layer in the OSI Model and the TCP/IP Model, highlighting their interactions and responsibilities in facilitating end-to-end communication.
  4. Explore the role of network protocols and standards in ensuring interoperability, compatibility, and security in communication networks.

Read

  1. Dordal, P.L. (2023). Introduction to Computer Networks - Second Edition (2nd ed.). http://intronetworks.cs.luc.edu/current2/html/index.html
    • View the online book.
      • Read Chapter 1 - An Overview of Networks

        The chapter “An Overview of Networks” covers fundamental aspects of networks including layers, data rate, throughput, bandwidth, packets, datagram forwarding, topology, and LANs with a special emphasis on Ethernet.

        • Section 1.1 – Layers
        • Section 1.2 – Data Rate, Throughput and Bandwidth
        • Section 1.3 – Packets
        • Section 1.4 – Datagram Forwarding
        • Section 1.5 – Topology
        • Section 1.9 – LANs and Ethernet
      • Read Chapter 5 - Other LANs

        The chapter “Other LANs” focuses the shift to exploring different types of Local Area Networks (LANs), with section 5.5 specifically delving into Asynchronous Transfer Mode (ATM) networks.

        • Section 5.5 - Asynchronous Transfer Mode: ATM
      • Read Chapter 7 – Packets

        The chapter “Packets” concentrates on the concept of packets, discussing packet delay, variability in packet delay, and packet size, all crucial aspects in understanding the behavior and performance of packet-switched networks.

        • Section 7.1 - Packet Delay
        • Section 7.2 - Packet Delay Variability
        • Section 7.3 - Packet Size
