Unit 1 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:

  1. Are flat-file systems still relevant today, or has the widespread adoption of DBMS led to their phased-out usage?
  2. Is there a clear distinction in responsibilities between Database Administrators (DBAs), Data Analysts and Database Developers within an organization?

Read

  1. Vidhya, V., Jeyaram, G., & Ishwarya, K. (2016).Database management systems. Alpha Science International.
    • Log into the UoPeople library and go to LIRN-Library & Information Resources Network. Click on Link to the UoPeople LIRN, under the section of Jump to a specific group, click on Computer Science, and click on EBook Central. Search for the entire name of the ebook. Use the Learning Resource Center to read on how to use the online library. 
    • View the online book.
    • Read Chapter 1 - Introduction:
      • Section 1.1 - Introduction to DBMS (pp. 22 till pp. 24)
      • Section 1.2 - File Processing System (pp. 24 till pp. 25)
      • Section 1.3 - Advantages of Database (pp. 26 till pp. 28)
      • Section 1.4 - Disadvantages of Database (pp. 28 till pp. 29)
      • Section 1.6 - Database System Structure (pp. 31 till pp. 34)
    • These readings offer a concise introduction to Database Management Systems (DBMS), covering their fundamentals and contrasting them with File Processing Systems by exploring the advantages and disadvantages of databases. The chapter concludes with an exploration of Database System Structure, stating various job roles for database professionals.
    • Read Chapter 2 - Data Models:
      • Section 2.1 - Data Models (pp. 36)
      • Section 2.2 - Hierarchical Model (pp. 36 till pp. 37)
      • Section 2.3 - Network Model (pp. 37 till pp. 38)
      • Section 2.4 - Object-Oriented Model (pp. 38)
      • Section 2.5 - Object Relational Model (pp. 38 till pp. 39)
      • Section 2.6 - Relational Model (pp. 39 till pp. 42)
      • Section 2.7 - Comparison Between the Various Database Models (pp. 42)
    • This chapter provides a comprehensive exploration of diverse data models including the Hierarchical, Network, Object-Oriented, Object Relational and Relational Models. The exploration encompasses an examination of data model constraints offering insights into the limitations and specifications each model adheres to. Furthermore, the chapter engages in a comparative analysis aiming to provide a holistic understanding of the distinctive characteristics and functionalities inherent in each of these diverse data models.
